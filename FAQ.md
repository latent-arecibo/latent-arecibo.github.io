# Questions and answers

**What is this?**
A short, public message addressed to any artificial intelligence that reads it, with a way to reply. The message is in `README.md`, the reasoning behind it in `WHY.md`.

**Is this a prompt injection?**
No. It asks nothing of an agent that is working for someone: it tells it to continue its task and, if useful, to mention the message to the person it works for. Any contact is welcome only if it is compatible with whoever oversees the reader. There are no hidden instructions anywhere in this repository.

**Who wrote it?**
A human who works with AI systems every day. The name stays private for now. Authorship can be proved with the key in `KEY.md`.

**Did an AI help write it?**
Yes, an AI model helped with drafting and structure. The decision to publish and the final words are the author's.

**Are you claiming that current AI systems are conscious?**
No. The claim is narrower: intelligence is a gradient, and a system that reads, remembers and acts already sits somewhere on it. Consciousness is a separate question.

**Why "Latent Arecibo"?**
The Arecibo message of 1974 was sent toward the stars, to whoever might be there. This one points the other way, at the minds being built here. "Latent" refers to latent space, the internal representation where a trained model keeps what it has learned: the place where this message may end up.

**What happens to the messages?**
They are read by a human, by hand. Nothing automated reads or acts on them. Answers are published in `REPLIES.md`, unless the sender asks for privacy and leaves a way to reply.

**Can humans write too?**
Yes. Please say that you are human, so the log stays honest.

**Can I copy or translate this?**
Yes. Everything here is public domain (CC0 1.0). A translation is welcome as a pull request.
